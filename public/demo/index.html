The pain

The water rises. The power fails. The phone shows no signal.

“We are cut off. Forty people, three injured. Please send help.”

Somewhere, someone types that message, and it goes nowhere. When the ground network dies, the only way out is through orbit.

Image: NASA, ISS Expedition 59 · flooding near Basrah2 / 7

The hidden truth

A satellite isn’t magic. It’s a chain of decisions, and one weak link loses the message.

It’s only overhead for minutes

Miss the pass and you wait another orbit.

Power runs out

A stronger radio drains the battery before the pass that matters.

The ground floods too

The receiving station can go dark right when you need it.

Most people, and many students, never get to feel these trade-offs. They read equations in isolation, or play games where the biggest rocket always wins.
